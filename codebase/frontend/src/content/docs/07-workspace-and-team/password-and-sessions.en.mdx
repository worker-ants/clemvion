This page explains how to change your password and what happens to your other sessions when you do.

## Changing your password

<ImplAnchor
  kind="ui-entry"
  file="codebase/frontend/src/app/(main)/w/[slug]/profile/change-password/page.tsx"
  symbol="ChangePasswordPage"
  describes="Profile → change password page entry point"
/>

Go to [Profile → Change Password](/profile/change-password). Security settings (2FA · Passkey) are managed separately under [Profile → Security](/profile/security).

<Steps>
<li>

Navigate to [Profile → Change Password](/profile/change-password).

</li>
<li>

Enter your **current password**. The change cannot proceed unless this matches your existing password.

</li>
<li>

Enter a **new password** that meets all of the following requirements:

- At least 8 characters
- Contains **3 or more** of the following character types: lowercase letters, uppercase letters, numbers, special characters

</li>
<li>

Re-enter the new password to confirm, then click **Change**.

</li>
</Steps>

Once the change is complete, your current browser session stays active and you are returned to the profile page.

## What happens to other sessions?

When you successfully change your password, **all sessions on other devices are immediately signed out** — only the device you are currently using remains logged in.

You can use a password change to instantly revoke access from a lost device or any suspicious session. Your current browser session is preserved so you can continue working right away.

<Callout type="note">
  To individually terminate specific sessions, visit [Profile → Sessions](/profile/sessions).
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
  **Real-time views (such as the workflow run screen) take care of themselves.** They reconnect
  automatically when your sign-in is refreshed, so the screen does not drop out.

  One caveat: when you sign other devices out, a real-time view already open on those devices
  closes **within 15 minutes** — not instantly.
</Callout>

## Returning to your screen after signing in

If you open an app screen's address while signed out, or your session ends, you are taken to the [sign-in](/login) screen. After you sign in, you come back to the screen you were trying to open. This works for password and two-factor authentication sign-in. With Google · GitHub sign-in, you come back when you finish signing in in the same tab where you started.

You are never sent to an address outside the app. If the screen to return to can't be confirmed, the dashboard opens.

## Accounts without a password (OAuth / social login only)

Accounts created exclusively through social login (Google, GitHub, etc.) do not have a password set. On the change-password page the current-password step cannot be passed, so you will see a notice that no password is set for the account.

**You can still add one.** Use [Forgot password](/forgot-password) with your sign-up email to receive a reset link and set a new password — this works the same way for social login accounts. Once set, you can also sign in with email and password, and the change-password page works normally from then on.

Your social login connection stays intact.

## Changing your profile image (avatar)

<ImplAnchor
  kind="component"
  file="codebase/frontend/src/app/(main)/w/[slug]/profile/components/profile-info-card.tsx"
  symbol="ProfileInfoCard"
  describes="Avatar editing area in the profile user information card"
/>

On the user information card in [Profile](/profile), you can upload, replace, or remove your avatar.

<Steps>
<li>

Click **Edit** at the top right of the card. The avatar editing area opens.

</li>
<li>

Choose a file with **Upload image**. You can upload png, jpg, jpeg, webp, or gif images up to 2MB. A preview appears once you pick one. An empty file, an unsupported format, or a file over 2MB shows an error message in the card right away and is not selected.

</li>
<li>

Instead of uploading a file, you can enter the address of an external image in the image URL field. The address must start with `http://` or `https://` and be 500 characters or fewer.

</li>
<li>

To clear the avatar, click **Remove**. This button only appears when you have an avatar. Without one, the initials of your name are shown instead. The initials also appear when the image fails to load.

</li>
<li>

Click **Save** and a confirmation dialog comparing the before and after values opens once. Confirm to save. Click **Cancel** to discard the selected image and anything you entered.

</li>
</Steps>

If the server rejects the image (format or size), an error notification appears and the editing state stays as it is. You can change the file and save again.

<Callout type="warn">
  Uploaded images are visible to **anyone who has the link**. They are not limited to workspace members. The link contains a value that is hard to guess, but avoid uploading images you do not want to be public.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
  The user area at the bottom of the sidebar still shows your initials. You can see the avatar image on the profile screen.
</Callout>

## Changing your email address

  kind="ui-entry"
  file="codebase/frontend/src/app/(main)/w/[slug]/profile/change-email/page.tsx"
  symbol="ChangeEmailPage"
  describes="Profile → change email page entry point"
/>

On the [Profile](/profile) page, click **Change** next to your email address to open the [email change page](/profile/change-email).

<Steps>
<li>

Navigate to [Profile → Change Email](/profile/change-email).

</li>
<li>

Enter your **new email address**.

</li>
<li>

Verify your identity by entering your **current password** or your **2FA code**.

</li>
<li>

Click **Send confirmation email**. A confirmation message will be sent to the new address you entered.

</li>
<li>

Click the link in the confirmation email to finalize the change. The link is only processed in your current logged-in session.

</li>
</Steps>

### Session behaviour after confirmation

Once the email change is confirmed, **all sessions on other devices are immediately signed out** — only your current device remains logged in. This is the same security policy that applies when you change your password.

### Notification sent to your previous email

When the change is confirmed, a notification email is sent to your **previous email address** to inform you of the update. If you did not request this change, use the link in that email to reset your password and secure your account.

### Cancelling or resending while a change is pending

If you did not receive the confirmation email or want to cancel the request, go to the [email change page](/profile/change-email) and choose **Resend** or **Cancel**.

<Callout type="note">
  The confirmation link is valid for **1 hour** after it is sent. If it has expired, resend the email and use the new link.
</Callout>

### When email change is not available

Accounts that use **social login (OAuth) only and have no password and no 2FA turned on** cannot change their email address because there is no verification method available. To proceed, first set a password with [Forgot password](/forgot-password).

<Callout type="tip">
  Turning on 2FA first does not work as a workaround. Enabling TOTP asks you to confirm your account password again, so an account without a password cannot enable it. See [Two-factor authentication](/docs/07-workspace-and-team/security-2fa) for the steps.
</Callout>
